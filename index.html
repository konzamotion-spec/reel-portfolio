How Icut

  • Dead air, retakes and filler go first.The speech stays natural, just tighter.
  • Captions land word by word, one accent per line.Readable in half a second, even with the sound off.
  • Graphics take the full screen or sit above the speaker.Nothing covers the face.
  • Every visual change gets a sound.Whooshes, hits and textures mixed under the voice.
